Your data and enquiries

This notice explains how information you send through the LLC Electroservice website is used.

Information you provide

The form collects your name, phone number and an optional message. These details are used to respond to your enquiry and discuss cooperation.

How enquiries are sent

Messages are sent through the third-party service FormSubmit for delivery by email. Submission only happens when you complete and confirm the form. You may instead contact office@elservis.com.ua or call us directly.

External links and technical data

The map and catalogue open on third-party services only when you choose their links. The hosting provider may process technical request logs, including your IP address and browser details, to operate and protect the website.

Questions about your data

Contact office@elservis.com.ua to ask about processing, retention periods, correction or deletion of your data. Do not include confidential information that is not needed for your enquiry.
